## Part B: Cloud Cost Analysis

- The first scenario cost $19.92 per month. The second cost $36,266.52 per month. I did find that second number surprising. I would assume that maintaining a heavy analytical workload would be quite costly. I just hadn't imagined it costing the same as a mid-level sedan.
- I hadn't realized how many offerings were directly designed for other companies/software. Trying to set up a simple version of one product would take longer at times as I had to sift through all of the custom versions to find what I wanted.
- The second scenario is much greater in power and expense, and understanding the needs of your project in order to select the needed GPU is quite important in keeping this cost effective.
